# Physical validation — 2026-08-25

CR20KB reports a successful real-world PETG HF test of the Snap-Fit Holder on a long cylindrical object. Snap-in effort and retention were judged excellent in handling.

Width decision from physical use:
- for object diameters **D >= 20 mm**, the recommended/default axial holder width is **20 mm**;
- for D < 20 mm, the default follows D with a 12 mm minimum;
- users can increase width manually when needed;
- long or heavy objects are better supported by multiple holders than by one unnecessarily wide holder.

The first calibrated baseline remains: object OD 22.03 mm, clip ID 22.00 mm, PETG HF, desired Normal snap effort.
